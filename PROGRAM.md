# Your Training Program

**Schedule:** every other day, 30–40 min · **Equipment:** none (a doorframe, chair, couch, and later a sturdy table)
**Goal:** muscle & strength + general fitness · **Kegel:** daily, ~6 min (general health + sexual performance)

## The week

Workouts alternate **A → B → A → B**, one every other day (about 3–4 per week). Kegel every day.

| Day | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Training | **A** + Kegel | Kegel | **B** + Kegel | Kegel | **A** + Kegel | Kegel | **B** + Kegel | … |

## Session structure (~35 min)

1. **Warm-up — 4.5 min:** jumping jacks, arm circles, hip circles, cat-cow, inchworms, slow squats, scapular push-ups, glute bridges
2. **Main work — ~25 min:** 4 exercises × 3 sets, 75 s rest between sets
3. **Cool-down — 3 min:** chest, hip flexor, hamstring stretches, child's pose
4. **Kegel — ~6 min**

## How you progress (the core of calisthenics)

- Every set: aim for the **top** of the range with good form; stop 1–2 reps before failure.
- When you hit the **top of the range on all 3 sets**, the app offers to move you to the next, harder variation.
- Rep counts drop after leveling up. That is expected, and it's how you keep building muscle without weights.

| Track | Ladder |
|---|---|
| Push | Incline → **Push-up** → Decline → Diamond → Archer → Pseudo-planche |
| Legs | **Squat** → Split squat → Bulgarian split squat → Assisted shrimp → Assisted pistol → Pistol |
| Pull | **Doorframe row** → Single-arm doorframe row → Table row (bent knees → straight → feet elevated) |
| Core | **Plank** → Tuck hollow → Hollow hold → Hollow rocks |
| Shoulders | **Pike push-up** → Elevated pike → Wall handstand hold → HSPU negatives → Wall HSPU |
| Glutes/hams | **Glute bridge** → Single-leg bridge → Single-leg hip thrust → Nordic curl negatives |
| Back | **Superman hold** → Prone Y-T-W → Reverse snow angels → Superman pulls |
| Lower abs | **Knee raise** → Leg raise → Reverse crunch → Dragon flag negatives |

## Kegel protocol

Each session = **3 rounds** of slow holds + quick flicks, then **5 reverse-Kegel breaths** (active relaxation, which is important for sexual function and control).

| Level | Slow holds | Quick flicks / round | When to move up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Foundation | 10 × 3 s squeeze / 3 s relax | 10 | after 14 sessions (~2 weeks) |
| 2 Building | 10 × 5 s / 5 s | 10 | after 14 sessions |
| 3 Endurance | 10 × 8 s / 8 s | 15 | after 14 sessions |
| 4 Mastery | 10 × 10 s / 10 s | 20 | keep going; progress to sitting and then standing |

Squeeze **up and in** (as if stopping urine and holding gas). Keep belly, glutes and thighs relaxed and keep breathing.
Don't train while actually urinating. Expect noticeable results in 4–12 weeks.

## Muscle-building basics

- **Protein:** about 1.6 g per kg of bodyweight daily. **Sleep:** 7–9 h. Rest days are when muscle grows.
- If a workout is rated "Too hard" twice in a row, drop that exercise one level in Settings.
- Every 8 weeks, take an easy week: do 2 sets instead of 3.
- Joint pain (not muscle burn)? Stop that exercise and regress a level.

*This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor if you have any health conditions.*
